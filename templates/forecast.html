How the forecast works ({{ ml_engine }}). Monthly unit sales of each product are divided by a store-wide seasonal index, a linear trend is fitted to the de-seasonalised series, the trend is projected 3 months ahead (capped at ±6% of average demand per month so one unusual month cannot run away), and the seasonal index is applied again. Recommended stock = next month's forecast + safety stock (1.65 × the standard deviation of the model's past errors, about a 95% service level). Accuracy is a back-test: the model is trained without the last 3 months and then scored on them (100% − weighted absolute percentage error). The seasonal index comes from a single year of history, so treat forecasts as planning estimates and refresh them as new sales arrive.
