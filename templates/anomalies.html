How detection works. Each expense is compared with similar past expenses (same description when there are at least 6 of them, otherwise the whole category). A robust Z-score is calculated as (amount − median) ÷ (1.4826 × median absolute deviation). An expense is flagged when the score is 3.5 or more and it is at least 1.5× the typical amount; 3× or more is High severity. Median-based statistics are used so that one large bill does not hide another. Stock purchases are excluded because they scale with sales.
